**Subject:** Quotation and technical data request — 83 references (SJ Wheels, Spain)

Dear [nombre],

We are SJ Wheels, an alloy wheel retailer based in Spain. We are preparing our
first product launch and have selected 83 of your references, grouped into 10
designs. The full list, with the diameter, width, bolt pattern, offset and
centre bore we hold for each one, is in the attached spreadsheet
(`solicitud-proveedor-83-skus.csv`). Please complete the empty columns and
return it.

Before we can publish or order anything, we need the following in writing.

**1. Prices**
- Price per single wheel, and price per set of four, for each reference.
- Currency.
- How long the price is valid.
- Incoterm (EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP…) and the named place.

**2. Order conditions**
- Minimum order quantity: per reference, per design and for the whole order.
- Whether we may mix references within one order, and any surcharge for doing so.

**3. Availability and lead times, stated separately**
- Current availability per reference.
- Production / preparation lead time in working days.
- Transit time in days, quoted separately from the preparation time.

**4. Packing data — we need all five figures**
- Net weight of one wheel (kg).
- Number of boxes per set of four.
- Gross weight of each box (kg), packaging included.
- Box dimensions (L × W × H, cm).
- Whether the goods ship palletised or as loose boxes.

**5. Shipping**
- Delivery cost to mainland Spain, to the Balearic Islands and to the Canary
  Islands, stating the quantity each quote is based on.
- Which costs are included in your quote and which are not: export clearance,
  freight, insurance, import duty, VAT/IGIC, customs handling, last-mile
  delivery, unloading.
- Please note the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla are outside the EU VAT area
  and require customs formalities even when shipped from mainland Spain.

**6. Finishes**
Your references use the codes MB, MG, MBI, MIB, B, YBZ and MB+L, and elsewhere
in your catalogue the spelled-out names MATTE BLACK and MATT GUNMETAL MACHINE
appear as separate codes. We do not want to guess. Please send your finish chart
with the code, the commercial name and a colour sample or photograph for each.

**7. What is included with the wheels**
- Are tyres included? Centre caps? Bolts or nuts? Hub-centric rings?
- If any of these are optional, please price them separately.

**8. Technical documentation**
For each reference, or for each design if that is how you hold it:
- Load rating (kg per wheel) and the PCD/offset/centre bore you certify.
- Test reports and approvals that apply: TÜV, JWL, VIA, ISO 9001, any other.
- Material and manufacturing process (gravity cast, flow formed, forged).

**9. Photographs**
The images we currently have are between 120 and 930 pixels wide, which is too
small for a product page. Please send the original high-resolution files for the
10 designs — at least 2000 px on the long side — including a second view and,
where you have one, a photograph of the wheel fitted to a vehicle.

**10. Claims**
What is your procedure if a wheel arrives damaged or out of specification: the
deadline to report it, the evidence you need and who pays the return freight.
